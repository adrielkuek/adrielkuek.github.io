# Adriel Kuek

**AI Researcher · Technical Leader · Builder of Slightly Over-Engineered Thought Spirals**

I work at the intersection of AI research and technical leadership — which is a fancy way of saying I spend an unhealthy amount of time thinking about optimisation problems, research strategy, and how to build the next operationally impactful AI capability.

I’m currently a **Lab Director** and **Principal Member of Technical Staff** at **DSO National Laboratories**, where I lead research efforts in Foundation Models and emerging AI technologies for defence and national security applications. Over the past 15+ years, I’ve wandered through signal processing, embedded systems, large-scale video analytics, and eventually into the wonderfully chaotic world of AI and machine learning.

These days, my interests revolve around sovereign multimodal and language foundation models, agentic AI, autonomous research systems, world models, and reasoning architectures. I’m particularly interested in how we move AI models from mere “next-token statistical predictors” toward systems that are more adaptive, collaborative, and genuinely useful in complex environments.

I’ve had the privilege of mentoring students on AI research projects that secured recognition at both **SSEF** and **ISEF**, which constantly reminds me that curiosity scales faster than compute budgets. Beyond student mentorship, I also work closely with research interns ranging from pre-university to PhD attachments. When we are not running experiments or preparing for rebuttals, our brainstorming sessions drift between research musings and random debates on hypothetical use cases.

This site is my little corner of the internet for collective experience, mini experiments, publications, research ideation, and the occasional over-engineered thought spiral. Some posts may be polished and structured. Others may look like a hastily scripted Markdown file. Both are part of the process.

Outside of work, I enjoy photography, travel vlog creation, hiking, and exploring obscure Instagrammable cafés with my family. These days, I mostly shuffle between paper reading, vibing on Claude Code CLI, and Netflix sessions with the wife — usually while telling myself I’ll “just test one more idea” before sleeping, a strategy that continues to fail with remarkable consistency.
