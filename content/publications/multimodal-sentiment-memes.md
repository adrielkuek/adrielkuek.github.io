abstract: "Explores methods for detecting hateful memes from the Facebook Hateful Memes Dataset. Tests BERT, BERT with RAG, VGG19, VGG19 with pretraining, and UNITER. Finds BERT with RAG performs best (0.842 AUC, 0.788 accuracy), likely due to additional information from RAG. Text-based models outperform image-based models, suggesting images alone contain insufficient inferable information."
