abstract: "Introduces SINDEX, a framework for Singapore-specific multimodal misinformation detection. Features automated evidence retrieval via H2-keywordextractor and SBERT querying NewsAPI, a CLIP-based multimodal truthfulness classifier, a ChatGPT-4-involved detection system, and a custom SBERT-based satire detector. Achieves strong F1-scores across verification, satire detection, and explanation generation."
