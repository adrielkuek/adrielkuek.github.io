abstract: "Introduces MERMAID, a dataset of 3,633 memes annotated with entities and relations, and proposes MERF, a pipeline that extracts entities and their relationships in memes. Combines NLP and computer vision to extract text and image features and infer relationships. Establishes a benchmark for multimodal meme semantic understanding, including evaluation in low-resource settings."
