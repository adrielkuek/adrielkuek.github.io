abstract: "Revisits Dynamic Scene Graph Generation (DSGG) from task setup and model design perspectives, introducing five complementary evaluation measures beyond recall. Combines a top-down reason-then-locate strategy with Temporal Relation Set prediction and Importance-Aware Finetuning to generate informative and diverse relations. Temporal Relation Sets deliver up to 3.7× faster training and 8.6× faster inference than the frame-wise MLLM baseline, while importance-aware finetuning improves relation ranking. Achieves state-of-the-art results on Action Genome, VidVRD, and PVSG."
