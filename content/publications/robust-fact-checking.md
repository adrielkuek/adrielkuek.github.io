abstract: "Formalizes Fact-Checking under Contamination (FCC) and proposes DEDUCE, a verification architecture for adversarial knowledge environments. Introduces claim decomposition with dynamic evidence-weighting: evidence reliability is treated as a latent variable, iteratively updated through agreement-based feedback. Demonstrates robustness against topically aligned, adversarially generated distractors, significantly narrowing the gap between clean and contaminated knowledge retrieval."
