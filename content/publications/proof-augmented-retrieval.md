abstract: "Proposes Path-Augmented Retrieval and Reasoning (PARR), a framework for training generative language models for Knowledge Graph Completion. Uses grounding paths from interpretable link predictors to provide retrieval and reasoning supervision: augmenting retrieval, supervising Rewriter LLM for query rewritings, and distilling structural knowledge to a Reasoner LLM via chain-of-thoughts. First native LLM-based framework to surpass traditional discriminative KGC methods across both transductive and inductive settings."
