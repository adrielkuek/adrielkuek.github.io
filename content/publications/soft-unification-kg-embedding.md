abstract: "Integrates Neural Theorem Provers (NTPs) with Knowledge Graph Embedding (KGE) methods to combine end-to-end differentiability with interpretability. Leverages structural learning in KGEs to improve NTP embedding space and substitutes NTPs with efficient KGE operations, reducing evaluation time by over 1000x on large-scale datasets such as WN18RR with a mild accuracy trade-off."
