Late in announcing, but not abating in excitement — I'm glad to see through the hard work of **Yuriel Ryan**, together with **Hei Man Ip**, **Paul Liang** and **Roy Ka-Wei Lee** in the acceptance of our paper:

> **"Self-Captioning Multimodal Interaction Tuning: Amplifying Exploitable Redundancies for Robust Vision Language Models"**
> *Proceedings of the International Conference on Machine Learning (ICML) 2026*
> [arXiv:2605.08145](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2605.08145)

We've been working on combating hallucination (Hei Man's work in multimodal memes) and understanding (Ryan's work in humor and sarcasm analysis) in our VLMs for a while now. Putting on the **information theoretic lens** to the problem provides a fresh take on how we can better leverage interaction tuning on multimodal representations to improve model consistency.

Happy for Yuriel on hitting this first major milestone for a Tier-1 publication while juggling his Masters. Definitely not a small feat — I'm confident in his amazing potential and trajectory for a lustre research journey ahead.
